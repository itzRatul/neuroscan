Five phases.
Sixteen seconds.

Each step targets a specific muscle group that stroke commonly affects.

Step by step

The 16-second journey.

01
00:00 → 00:04
4 s · auto
REST

Face natural, look straight at the camera. Sets your per-person landmark baseline.

Every later reading is a delta from this frame.

02
00:04 → 00:07
3 s · action
SMILE

Smile naturally. Measures the upward pixel delta of each mouth corner.

The "F" in FAST — drooping mouth corner is the strongest stroke sign.

03
00:07 → 00:10
3 s · action
EYES CLOSE

Close both eyes gently. Measures closure ratio vs rest on each side.

Cranial nerve VII — affected side may fail to fully seal (lagophthalmos).

04
00:10 → 00:13
3 s · action
EYES OPEN

Open eyes as wide as possible. Measures opening height on each side.

Asymmetric ptosis (drooping eyelid) is a classic stroke indicator.

05
00:13 → 00:16
3 s · action
SMILE AGAIN

One last smile. Compared against phase 02 to confirm reproducibility.

Two consistent readings rule out a single-frame glitch.

Action-triggered timing. Each 3-second window starts only after NeuroScan detects you performing the action — not when the instruction appears.
