Clinical paper

The medical basis for the scoring.

01
Cincinnati Prehospital Stroke Scale: reproducibility and validity
Kothari, R. U., Pancioli, A., Liu, T., Brott, T., & Broderick, J. · Annals of Emergency Medicine, 33(4), 373–378
Used for: the 50 / 25 / 25 score weighting (smile vs. eye close vs. eye open). Facial droop is the most sensitive CPSS sign.
1999 DOI
Training datasets

Where the thresholds come from.

02
Annotated Facial Images for Stroke Classification
Elhanashy, A. · Kaggle · 2,490 stroke + 5,000 normal facial images
Used for: calibrating score thresholds — Normal ≤30, Moderate >30, High Risk >60.
Dataset Kaggle
03
Facial Droop and Facial Paralysis Images
Mehta, K. · Kaggle · 1,024 labelled palsy images across severity spectrum
Used for: tuning eye-close ratio (0.35) and eye-open ratio (1.25).
Dataset Kaggle
